PRIVACY, WITHOUT THE LEGAL-SPEAK

What can the Teacher see?

The short answer: mission context, not your whole computer.

✓

AUTOMATIC

Teacher sees automatically

  • Current lesson and mission
  • Your answers and decisions
  • The connected Codex conversation
  • Relevant file changes and tests
ASK

ONLY WITH PERMISSION

Teacher sees only when you share

  • One window or screenshot
  • Selected or unsaved text
  • Your voice during an active call
×

NEVER

Teacher never sees

  • Passwords, tokens, or secret settings
  • Unrelated applications or browser tabs
  • Other Codex conversations or projects
  • Continuous hidden screen capture
  • Approvals made on your behalf
