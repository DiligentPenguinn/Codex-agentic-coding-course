System overview

The companion coordinates learning while Codex remains the real work surface. Context crosses boundaries only through named, inspectable adapters.

  1. 01
    Learner controls the loop

    Prompts, approvals, and decisions stay learner-owned.

  2. 02
    One mission binds to one thread

    No window-title guessing or unrelated conversation access.

  3. 03
    Context is assembled, not copied wholesale

    Every dynamic field has scope, source, and freshness.

TEACHER NEVER GETS

Course-aware, not surveillance.

  • × Secrets, tokens, or raw environment variables
  • × Unbound Codex threads or other repositories
  • × Unrelated applications or browser tabs
  • × Silent continuous screen capture
  • × Authority to approve Executor actions

Screen capture is always learner-triggered, source-specific, visible while active, and revocable.

01

ROLE CONTRACT

Teacher coaches. Executor operates.

Teacher Chat may recommend a prompt, but only the learner sends or approves work in Codex.

02

IDENTITY CONTRACT

Explicit thread binding beats focus detection.

Every event and context read must match the active course run's persisted threadId.

03

EVIDENCE CONTRACT

“Done” is not completion.

Checkpoints use observable artifacts, diffs, tests, and learner-owned answers.
